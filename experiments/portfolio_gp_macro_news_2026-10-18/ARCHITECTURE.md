# Design frozen before evaluation

Existing modules reviewed: `stock_api/heteroskedastic.py` implements Matérn-3/2 drift and log-variance GPs, collapsed q(f)q(g), regularized two-start hyperparameter optimization, `_future` full temporal covariance, and simulation. `stock_api/portfolio.py` supplies `joint_gp`, shrunk residual dependence, constrained mean–variance optimization, exact self-financing costs, and CRPS. `scripts/run_portfolio_gp_22.py` provides ticker extraction, monthly execution conventions, daily accounting and performance metrics. Original reports/configuration/cache were read; none will be overwritten.

Both windows reset price/GP histories. Monthly origins are the session before the first trading day on/after the 17th. July evaluation starts on that rebalance, not July 1; January 2023 ends January 31 with a partial final holding. Window B ends October 16, so its final allocation is September 17. Warm-up GP forecasts beginning as soon as 30 returns exist provide strictly earlier completed monthly forecast errors for incremental fitting. Final April–October 2026 comparison resets wealth/holdings separately and reuses the identical six original GP distributions after checking prices, horizon and code.

Six macro factors: changes in two-year yields, 10-year inflation compensation, CPI year-over-year inflation, asinh(WTI/50), unemployment, and log VIX. SPY contemporaneous returns control for market exposure. Each macro state comes from an ALFRED vintage dated the calendar day before the GP origin. Snapshot availability, observation date and vintage are retained; original release time is NOT invented. Actual changes are not market surprises. AR(1) forecasts of these changes, shrunk toward zero and fit only to completed intervals, supply the ex-ante factor forecasts. No future factor changes enter allocations. CPI/UNRATE vintage revisions known at the snapshot can affect observed state changes; these are changes in available information, not clean structural innovations.

Descriptive exposures fit observed monthly stock log returns on contemporaneous monthly factor changes plus SPY. Coefficients have Gaussian ridge posterior uncertainty, conditional on model assumptions, estimated independently in each window with up to 24 prior intervals. Fixed units/scales make transfer interpretable. Sparse early histories imply broad uncertainty, not causal identification. All data used in each fit precede that allocation.

B fits prior GP forecast errors on realized factor changes plus contemporaneous SPY, then predicts with ex-ante AR factor forecasts and zero expected SPY innovation. C regresses those same completed errors on news features available at their historical origins. D fits factor changes and historical-origin news jointly, so correlated predictors compete in one regularized mapping. This directly targets information incremental to price-only GP forecasts. Fixed ridge penalties are never selected on portfolio performance. At least three completed GP-error months are required; otherwise emit an explicitly flagged zero correction. Primary covariance stays unchanged for a matched test of expected-return information: parameter/AR uncertainty is estimated but NOT independently added to GP volatility, which already contains macro risk. Augmented forecasts are conditional plug-in distributions, not fully Bayesian joint macro/GP forecasts.

News rule: all available FOMC statements, plus fixed GDELT 2.0 event snapshots at noon UTC on the 5th/15th of every month. This selection is based on calendar/source rules, not returns. GDELT ingestion time is the known availability bound; event date is not publication date. Wait 24 hours after documented publication/ingestion. Deduplicate URLs and event identifiers. Extract deterministic policy direction, inflation attention, growth weakness, trade attention and conflict attention. No LLM keys/interface are configured; these features must never be described as LLM output or market surprises. Late-released FOMC transcripts/minutes are excluded from statement features. Geopolitical event descriptions are machine-coded CAMEO records, not invented article headlines. Coverage is incomplete.

Transfer freezes both Window A descriptive exposures and its GP-error exposure posterior, inflates posterior covariance by four, and caps additional prior precision at six standardized observations. Window B updates from its own completed intervals; no GP temporal state crosses the gap. Compare transfer vs weak-prior macro model. Placebos permute historical predictor rows within the available training sample with one permutation shared by all assets, preserving market-wide cross-sectional shocks and chronological eligibility; serial dependence is disrupted, so they are limited diagnostics.

All portfolios use existing lambda=10, 10 bp bought-plus-sold fees, $2,000, sum=1 and 20% caps. Gross replays chosen weights without fees. Daily closes determine volatility and drawdown. The existing log-return utility and previous-close forecast/next-open execution approximation remain unchanged. Three-month circular block bootstrap resamples entire market months, not independent stock-months. Report paired error differences and intervals without choosing winners or claiming causal attribution. Event-study dates are fixed by archival release/sampled event rules; major named events are descriptive post-event checks, never features before publication.

Estimated workload: roughly 65 monthly joint GP fits ×22 stocks, minus six reusable original forecasts; 10,000 paths each. Approximately 430 small ALFRED CSV snapshots, 140 small GDELT files, and 50–60 FOMC statements. Zero paid LLM calls. About 500–700 inexpensive portfolio solves. Cache input/code fingerprints; run one small end-to-end test before full evaluation.
